0.3778a - Activation of protection for newbies

Information on protection for newbies:
The protection is in effect for players with less than 10k points.
When the protection is on, you cannot attack active players who have a number of points
less than 10 times less than the attacker (highlighted in green in the galaxy),
Also, active players with 10 times more points than the attacker cannot be attacked
Examples:
Player A - 2k points, Player B < 200 points - both cannot attack each other
Player A - (20k), Player B (< 2k) - both cannot attack each other
Player A - (200k), Player B (< 10k) - both cannot attack each other
Player A - (200k), Player B (> 10k) - both can attack each other
Player A - (50k), Player B (8k) - both can attack each other
